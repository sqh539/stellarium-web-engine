Stars

Combination of the following catalogues:

  • Gaia DR2: Data from the ESA mission Gaia, processed by the Gaia DPAC. Gaia Collaboration et al. (2016, 2018b).
  • Hipparcos: ESA, 1997, The Hipparcos and Tycho Catalogues, ESA SP-1200
  • Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised Ed. (Hoffleit+, 1991)

Deep Sky Objects

  • HyperLeda database, Makarov et al. 2014, A&A, 570, A13
  • SIMBAD database, operated at CDS, Strasbourg, France. Wenger et al. 2000, A&AS, 143, 9
  • Open NGC Database: Mattia Verga (OpenNGC)

Background Image

Digitized Sky Survey — STScI/NASA, colored & healpixed by CDS. Based on DSS2-red (POSS-II F, AAO-SES, SR, SERC-ER) and DSS2-blue (POSS-II J, SERC-J, EJ).
